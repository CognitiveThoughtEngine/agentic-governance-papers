# Paper 1 — Decision Load Index (DLI)

**Title:** Decision Load Index: A Cognitive Load Diagnostic for AI-Augmented Workplaces

**Author:** Michael K. Saleme, Cognitive Thought Engine LLC

**Status:** Published

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.18217577](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18217577)

**Zenodo Record:** https://zenodo.org/record/18217577

---

## Abstract

Introduces the Decision Load Index — a composite metric for quantifying cognitive load in AI-augmented workplaces. As AI systems take over execution tasks while simultaneously generating new classes of oversight, correction, and coordination decisions, knowledge workers face a decision load increase that existing cognitive load frameworks do not measure. The DLI instrument provides a ten-item diagnostic that captures decision volume, decision complexity, decision reversibility, and decision time pressure across an individual's AI-augmented workflow. Validation data from 900+ users demonstrates that high DLI scores predict burnout, disengagement, and organizational friction at rates consistent with existing cognitive load research. The paper presents the DLI as a measurement foundation for AI-mediated work design and organizational assessment.

---

## Key Findings

- High DLI scores are a leading indicator of burnout and disengagement in AI-augmented workplaces, preceding self-reported burnout by an average of several weeks in longitudinal cohorts.
- The DLI captures a dimension of cognitive load not addressed by existing instruments: the load arising from AI oversight, correction, and coordination decisions — not from direct task execution.
- Organizational interventions targeting DLI reduction (decision batching, threshold-based escalation, AI decision logging) produce measurable reductions in reported burnout and friction.
